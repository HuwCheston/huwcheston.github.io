Jazz is a highly complex form of music. Great jazz musicians have a **unique style** that makes them recognisable: but how do we best show these styles?

Using a variety of machine learning and signal processing techniques, we have created an **interactive web application designed for music education** that shows off the styles of twenty famous jazz musicians. Our models use a variety of musical features and are trained on over 80 hours of audio recordings from our open-source [Jazz Trio Database](https://github.com/HuwCheston/Jazz-Trio-Database). We hope that this application will find use in **music education and teaching contexts.**

Our web application is written in **JavaScript** with the interactive visualisations powered by **Plotly** and **Magenta.js**. The machine learning models were trained in **Python** using **PyTorch** and **Scikit-Learn**.
